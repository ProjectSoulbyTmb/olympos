# Economy

Shop sell prices (you sell here): logs 8gp, oak_logs 20, copper_ore 6, tin_ore 6,
iron_ore 17, raw_shrimp 2, cooked shrimp 5.

Shop stock (one-off permanent tools):
| Tool | Price | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| iron_axe | 60 | +5% woodcutting success |
| steel_axe | 320 | +10% woodcutting success |
| iron_pickaxe | 60 | +5% mining success |
| steel_pickaxe | 320 | +10% mining success |

## Profit math
- One inventory trip of 28 logs sells for 224gp.
- An iron_axe pays for itself within one extra trip of logs.
- Cooked shrimp outsell raw shrimp 5 vs 2, and give cooking xp too - always cook
  before selling if you are near the range anyway.

The starting balance is 25 coins. Coins do not use inventory space.
